AvLights

A Roku screensaver showing live US aviation weather on FAA sectional charts.

AvLights displays real-time flight categories — VFR, MVFR, IFR and LIFR — as colour-coded dots positioned on FAA sectional charts and a national overview map. Weather updates automatically from the FAA Aviation Weather Center.

Chart assets

This site also hosts the sectional chart images the channel downloads at runtime, described by manifest.json. Charts are published as release assets and revised on the FAA's 56-day cycle.

Chart imagery is produced by the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration and is in the public domain. Weather data comes from the FAA Aviation Weather Center and NOAA's National Weather Service.
